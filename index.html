Aleksandra Gudkova

Composer & sound artist based in Austria.

bio

Aleksandra Gudkova is a composer and sound artist based in Austria, actively engaged in the contemporary music scene. She has performed at festivals including Orfeus, Kraa, V Novom Svetle, ARCHA+, NEXT, and participated in the international project Sound Experiments as well as the Veni Academy courses. She is a member of the Bratislava Improvisation Orchestra and CMKK. In recent years, her work has focused on the integration of diverse musical approaches. Her current artistic direction is shaped by an interest in modern technologies, softwares, sensors, physical objects and sociocultural practices.
